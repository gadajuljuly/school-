Delete Your Account - ITASK

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your ITASK account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Send an email to itask1305@gmail.com from any address, including the phone number registered on your account, and a short message asking to delete your account.

We will verify the request against the phone number on the account and process it within a reasonable time.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and phone number.
  • Your personal (non-shared) projects, tasks, reports, and site logs.
  • Files you uploaded to your own projects' library.
  • Your push-notification subscription/token, if any.

Shared projects

If you are a member of a shared project with other people, your individual account and login are deleted, but content you contributed to a shared project (tasks, chat messages, files) may remain visible to the other members of that project, since it is part of their shared project history. You may request that your own messages/content be removed from a shared project separately by contacting us at the address above.

Timeframe

Deletion requests are typically completed within 30 days of verification.

Questions

For any questions about this process, contact: itask1305@gmail.com
